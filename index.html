A quiet daily place between you and your kid.

TeenBridge is built for the years where the conversation gets harder — and the moments that used to be easy need a new shape.

What's in the app

  • Mood emoji, just for today — share with your partner or keep it private.
  • Question Box — sealed-envelope notes you can take back before they're read.
  • Daily Drop — a tiny new prompt every day.
  • Shared album — every photo passes content-safety review before your partner sees it.
  • Shared calendar with "surprise" events that stay hidden until the day arrives.
  • Two Truths and dad jokes — lightweight, deliberately unimportant.

The safety design

  • Sign in with Apple — we never see your email.
  • No clock times on shared content — only soft bands like "this afternoon."
  • Mood pattern analysis runs on your device, against your own log only.
  • Mutual-reveal reactions — your partner's reaction stays hidden until you've reacted too.
  • Crisis resources (988, Crisis Text Line, Childhelp) always visible, never blocking.
